The sweets are the one part of Halloween that actually run out mid-evening if you underestimate them, and nothing kills the mood on your street faster than switching the porch light off at 7pm. Here's what to look for when stocking up, and five options that cover everything from a big bulk box to smaller, allergy-conscious extras.

## What actually matters

- **Individually wrapped over loose pick 'n' mix.** Wrapped sweets are quicker to hand out, don't need tongs or gloves, and are far more reassuring to parents who'd rather not take anything that's been sitting loose in a bowl.
- **Enough volume for your street.** Work out roughly how many callers you get in a normal year and buy for that, not for a quiet night — running out and turning the porch light off early is worse than having a few pieces left over.
- **A resealable bag or box.** Anything left over keeps far better in a bag with a proper seal or a lidded box than in a torn-open multipack, so it's still fine to dip into after Halloween.
- **A mix of textures, not just chocolate.** Chocolate melts if it's a mild evening and gets messy on a doorstep table — a mix that includes gummies, fizzy sweets or lollies alongside chocolate holds up better and suits more kids' preferences.
- **Check for "may contain" allergen warnings.** Most bulk mixes aren't nut-free, so it's worth keeping a small separate allergy-friendly option alongside the main bowl.
