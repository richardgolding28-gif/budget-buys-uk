The garden and the windows are what trick-or-treaters and passers-by actually see, so it's worth putting the effort there rather than only decorating a hallway nobody walks past. Here's what makes outdoor Halloween decorations worth buying, and five picks that cover the front lawn, the porch, and the windows.

## What actually matters

- **Genuinely outdoor-rated, not just outdoor-photographed.** Look for a stated indoor/outdoor use claim rather than assuming — some lighter card or fabric pieces are indoor-only despite spooky styling.
- **Stakes or a stable base.** Anything going into a lawn or flower bed needs metal stakes or a weighted base, or the first breeze of the night knocks it flat.
- **Battery life and an on/off timer.** Battery-powered lanterns and lights that run all evening without you remembering to switch them off save both batteries and hassle.
- **Static cling over sticky adhesive for windows.** PVC clings come off cleanly; anything with actual glue risks residue or paint damage on rented or newly-painted frames.
- **Reusable rather than single-use.** Foam, plastic and PVC pieces that pack flat and survive storage are worth the slightly higher price over flimsier one-Halloween items.
