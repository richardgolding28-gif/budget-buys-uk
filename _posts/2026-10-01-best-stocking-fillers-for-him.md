Stocking fillers for him are easy to get wrong in a specific way: either too generic (another pair of plain socks) or too novelty (something funny for five minutes, then binned). The ones that land are either genuinely useful in his everyday routine or specifically, deliberately fun rather than just small and cheap. Here's what to look for, and five real options that cover grooming, a laugh, a drink, a gadget and a classic.

## What actually matters

- **Match the type to the person, not just the price.** A grooming set only works if he actually has a beard or routine to use it on; a gaming-themed item only lands if he games. Generic is safer, specific is better if you know him.
- **Mix useful with fun.** One practical item (grooming, a small gadget) alongside one purely novelty or treat item usually reads better in a stocking than five of the same type.
- **Check it's genuinely stocking-sized.** Gift sets can look small in a product photo but come in bulkier retail packaging than you'd expect — check the dimensions before assuming it'll fit.
- **Consumables are the safest bet for anyone you don't know well.** Drink miniatures, grooming products and sweets get used or drunk regardless of personal taste in novelty humour.
- **Don't rely on one "wow" item.** A stocking filled with two or three smaller, well-chosen things usually feels more generous than one slightly bigger item alone.
