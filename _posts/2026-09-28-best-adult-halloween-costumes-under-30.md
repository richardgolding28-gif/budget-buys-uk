Adult Halloween costumes fall into two camps at the budget end: genuinely wearable outfits that hold up for a night out, and thin, single-use novelty pieces that look fine in the product photo and nowhere else. Here's how to tell them apart, and five that are worth the money.

## What actually matters

- **One size vs. sized.** "One size fits most" costumes are usually cut generously and work best as an oversized layer over normal clothes, not a fitted outfit — check the size guide if fit actually matters to you.
- **What's actually included.** Cheaper listings often sell the main garment only, with the hat, belt, or accessories shown in the photo sold separately. Read the "what's in the box" section, not just the image.
- **Fabric weight for the weather.** Late October in the UK is cold — a thin poly-blend costume worn alone at an outdoor event will not be enough on its own.
- **Comfort for the whole night.** Anything with a boned bodice, a full mask, or restrictive sleeves is worth trying on early rather than discovering the problem at the party.
- **Reusability.** A classic (witch, vampire, skeleton) gets more wear over the years than a hyper-specific meme costume that dates within a season.
