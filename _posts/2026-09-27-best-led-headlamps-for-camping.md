Headlamps are one of the few categories where the cheap options are frequently just as good as the expensive ones, because the underlying LED and battery technology is commoditized. Here's what's actually worth paying attention to.

## What matters more than the lumen count

- **Red light mode** — preserves night vision and doesn't wreck everyone else's around the campfire. Almost every decent headlamp now includes it; treat its absence as a red flag.
- **Rechargeable vs. AAA** — rechargeable (USB) saves money over time and avoids carrying spare batteries, but AAA models can be "refueled" anywhere, which matters on longer trips off-grid.
- **IPX rating** — IPX4 or higher means it'll survive rain and sweat. Anything unrated is a gamble.
- **Motion sensor / hands-free controls** — a genuine convenience when your hands are full setting up camp or cooking, though it adds one more thing that can occasionally misfire.
