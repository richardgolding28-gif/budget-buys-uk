Stocking fillers for her are easy to get wrong in the same way gifts for him are: either too generic (another bar of soap) or too specific to a taste you're guessing at. The ones that land tend to be either genuinely pamper-worthy or quietly practical enough to get used every day through winter. Here's what to look for, and five real options that cover pampering, beauty, home fragrance, cosiness and a treat.

## What actually matters

- **Match the type to how she actually unwinds.** A bath and body set only works if she actually takes baths; a candle only works if she burns candles at home. Generic self-care items are safer than anything requiring specific taste.
- **Mix pampering with something practical.** One indulgent item (a gift set, a candle) alongside one everyday-useful item (socks, a sleep mask) tends to read better in a stocking than five of the same type.
- **Check it's genuinely stocking-sized.** Gift sets and hampers often photograph small but arrive in bulkier retail packaging than expected — check the dimensions before assuming it'll fit.
- **Consumables and toiletries are the safest bet for anyone you don't know well.** Bath products, candles and treats get used regardless of personal taste in jewellery or colour.
- **Don't rely on one "wow" item.** A stocking filled with two or three smaller, well-chosen things usually feels more generous than one slightly bigger item alone.
