Electric blankets are one of the cheapest ways to stay warm through a UK winter without turning the heating up — most underblankets cost only a penny or two per hour to run. The tricky part is knowing whether you want an underblanket (goes on the bed, under your sheet) or a heated throw (sits on top of you on the sofa), and which features are actually worth paying extra for. Here's what to look for, and which ones we'd buy.

## What actually matters

- **Underblanket vs. heated throw** — an underblanket warms the bed before you get in and is meant to be slept on; a heated throw is for sitting under on the sofa. They're not interchangeable, so check which one a listing actually is before buying.
- **Dual control on double/king sizes** — if you share a bed, a single-zone blanket means one of you is always too hot or too cold. Dual control lets each side be set independently.
- **Heat settings and an auto-off timer** — more settings give finer control, and a timer (commonly 1/3/9 hours or similar) means it won't run all night if you fall asleep with it on.
- **Overheat protection and safety marks** — check the product listing for overheat protection and any safety approval the retailer states (for example BEAB approval, a UK-specific safety mark for electric blankets). Don't rely on a blanket that doesn't state this anywhere.
- **Machine washable with a detachable control** — you can't wash the control unit, so it needs to unplug from the fabric before it goes in the machine.
