Trail & Tarp publishes short, practical buying guides for budget and beginner camping gear — tents, sleeping bags, coolers, lighting, and cooking gear.

We don't run a physical gear-testing lab. Our guides are researched from publicly available specifications, current pricing, and aggregate customer review data, and are written to help you compare options quickly rather than read fifteen tabs. Where we're not confident a claim is well-supported, we say so or leave it out.

If you buy something through a link on this site, we may earn a small commission — see our [affiliate disclosure](/disclosure/) for the details. It doesn't change the price you pay.

Questions or a correction to something we've published? Get in touch.
